2. The design, locked (Design & Brand summary — binding on every file)

DESIGN SYSTEM — The brand is "The Horizon": one line of light is the mark, the progress bar and the moment of sending; nothing else glows. Colour roles: ground #0D0B10; surface #17141C; raised #201B27; hairline rgba(246,241,234,0.10); text #F6F1EA; text-muted #B9B1A6; text-dim #8C857C (icons and ≥ 18 pt only); action #FFB547 with #1B1206 text on it; signal #FF5E3A (the line and its glow only, never text); danger #FF7A6B; success #5FD39A. Backgrounds are flat ground — the horizon gradient appears on three screens only (Welcome, questionnaire header, send). Type: Space Grotesk 700 at 40/44, 30/36, 22/28 and 500 at 18/24; Inter 16/24 body, 14/20 small, 13/18 label, 11/16 overline +1.6 tracking; one display size per screen; text scales to 160%. Spacing on a 4 pt base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64; gutters 20 pt (16 pt under 360 pt wide); card padding 20; section gap 32. Shape: radius 14 cards, 20 sheets, pill buttons 56 pt high; hairlines, no shadows. Motion: press spring damping 18 / stiffness 220; enter 260 ms fade + 12 pt rise; exit 160 ms; the horizon moves only in response to the person. Reduced motion: 160 ms cross-fades and a still horizon. Haptics: selection on chips, light on buttons, success once on send, warning on errors, with an off switch. Icons: one outlined set at 24 pt; no emoji as interface. No sound.

KEY LAYOUTS — This is a native app, so "desktop" is tablet and foldable. Widths: compact < 600 pt (one column, content max 560); medium 600–839 (centred 560 column; landscape puts the hero left, form right); expanded ≥ 840 (two panes: list 360 pt + detail). (1) Welcome — phone: horizon top 55%, title and two actions bottom 45%; tablet: horizon left half, actions right. (2) Questionnaire step — phone: horizon header 96 pt with "step x of N" (64 pt on phones ≤ 667 pt tall), one question, answers, fixed footer with Back / Skip / Continue inside the bottom 96 pt; tablet: the same single 600 pt column, horizon full-bleed. (3) Consultant — phone: messages, a brief tray peeking 64 pt above the input that opens to 70% and never covers the input; tablet: chat left, brief always open at 360 pt right. (4) Brief and send — phone: grouped options with ticks and a sticky Send; tablet: summary left, options right. (5) Ticket detail — phone: status line as a short horizon with five stops, thread, composer; tablet: tickets list left, detail right. The owner inbox reuses layout 5.

GRAPHICS / 3D — 2D only. The horizon is one Skia runtime shader (@shopify/react-native-skia) [CONFIRM: installable in Expo Go on SDK 58]; fallback, used automatically when Skia is missing or the device is low-end, is the starter's LinearGradient with an opacity animation in the same colours and position. Cost limit ≤ 4 ms per frame on a mid-range Android; it runs only while its screen is focused and stops in the background, in Low Power Mode and under Reduce Motion (still frame). A hero video is optional and only with real footage from Travis: 8–10 s muted loop, 720×1280, ≤ 2.5 MB, graded to the palette, loaded after first draw. 3D is not used: it adds megabytes, drains battery and is the least reliable part of Expo Go.

COMPONENT STATES — Every list, thread, form and card ships four states. Empty: one sentence and one action ("Your brief is with Travis", "No problems reported — nice"). Loading: skeletons in the final layout's shape with a 1.2 s shimmer; never a lone spinner. Error: danger hairline, the cause in plain words, Retry, and nothing the person typed is lost. Success: one horizon pulse and one haptic. Forms: button disabled until valid, holds its width while "Sending…", errors under the field. Attachments: picking, shrinking, uploading with a ring, failed with Retry, done. Offline: one banner, "You're offline — your answers are saved on this phone"; sends are queued and say so. Placeholders look deliberate: a "SAMPLE" or "To be added" tag with a dashed action-colour hairline and muted text — never an empty card, and case-study slots stay hidden until real.

SIGNATURE MOMENT — "Golden hour". Through the questionnaire the horizon line is the progress: it travels as each step is answered. On "Request my free quote" the send starts immediately; the line then rises and floods the screen gold for 900 ms, fades over 120 ms, and settles as the header line of the dashboard, with one success haptic. A tap skips it; under Reduce Motion it is a 160 ms cross-fade to the confirmation.

PERFORMANCE BUDGET — A native app has no LCP; the equivalent target is first usable screen: ≤ 1.5 s warm start and ≤ 2.5 s cold start on a mid-range Android phone [measure on a real device before store builds]. Total JavaScript: Hermes bundle ≤ 4 MB. Layout stability (the CLS equivalent): no content moves after first draw — skeletons hold final sizes. Frame rate: 60 fps through the questionnaire, no more than two dropped frames in a row. Animation limit: one shader, ≤ 4 ms per frame, on three screens. No 3D. Lists virtualised from 20 rows. Fonts: two families, five weights, bundled.

REJECTED — 3D scenes (cost and reliability). A gradient on every screen (it is what made the last build look like a template). Blur panels everywhere (expensive on Android) — none in v1. A stock-footage hero or stand-in faces (trust). An animated onboarding carousel (delays the first question). Confetti or sound on send (off-brand). A human-looking consultant avatar (the consultant says it is an AI). Lottie packs (a dependency for generic motion).

FREE TIER AND OFFLINE — Still met. The demo runs fully offline with SAMPLE data and no accounts. Live limits to hold: Supabase free plan [CONFIRM today's database, storage, email and pausing limits on supabase.com/pricing before a pilot]; photos shrunk on the phone to ≤ 1.5 MB (1600 px), hard stop 10 MB per file and 5 files per ticket; no polling — realtime only while a ticket is open; analytics written in batches, at most one write a minute per device, no writes on simply viewing a screen; consultant capped at 60 turns per user per day [CONFIRM] plus an overall daily cap [LLM DAILY CAP], with the simulated consultant taking over when either is reached; resend of sign-in codes after 60 s. Offline: every screen reads the phone's cache first; questionnaire, brief and tickets queue and send once, exactly once, when the connection returns.
